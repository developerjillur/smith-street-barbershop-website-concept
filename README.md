# Smith Street BarberShop website concept

An independent, responsive homepage concept for the Fitzroy business at 115 Smith Street, designed by Jillur Rahman. This repository is a design demonstration. It is not the business's official website, does not accept bookings, and has not been approved by the business.

The address and service themes were researched from public business information on 28 September 2026. For current hours, contact details, services, and bookings, use the business's [Google listing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Smith+Street+BarberShop/@-37.8055496,144.9830222,17z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x6ad642ddfc12417b:0x85918da58a5fad66) or [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/smithstreetbarbershop/).

The published photographs are representative images from other locations. They do not show the Smith Street premises, its staff, its customers, or its work. See [PHOTO-CREDITS.md](PHOTO-CREDITS.md) for photo sources and license details. The bundled typefaces are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; their notices are in `assets/fonts/licenses/`.

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the static site. The page uses relative asset paths and has no build step.
